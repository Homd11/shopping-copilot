Prepared 6 October 2026. This is an assistant-authored semantic annotation pass for **100 Gemini-generated, exposed synthetic messages** alongside the existing 40-record batch. No independent human label review is claimed. CAP-02 remains open.

Original text and [intake bytes](../eval/datasets/capstone-v1/development/intake/gemini-batch-001.original.json.txt) are preserved; source SHA-256 is `0dd0b3d788dbb59e75373bd92b1d2e3a868e6aa8de103fb7acffa55509298ea7`.

## Interpretation and limits

Each of the 100 messages was interpreted individually; the original label was retained in intake, not mechanically mapped. The v2 schema fixes `draft_unreviewed`, null reviewer, false evaluation eligibility and pending duplicate review. Those constants remain unchanged: this document records the assistant pass, not independent approval. Owner-authorized synthetic pilot use is a separate release decision; it does not turn these records into gold or unseen evidence.

The source supplies ordered Shopper turns in 28 groups but no assistant replies, observed product lists, cart state, executed outcomes or sanitised Snapshots. Context-dependent records have `requires_context: true` and null reference/hash, explicitly permitted by v2. Null does not mean context-free. Prior Shopper wording can carry a stated constraint forward, but never proves an Action succeeded. No product IDs, DOM targets, context hashes or fixtures were fabricated. All acceptable-target lists remain empty.

`intent_only_eligible` means only that the top-level capability is justified by this message under the fixed Controlled Storefront task. Clear commands can remain text-classification candidates despite unavailable execution context. Referential fragments, disputed labels and compound capabilities are excluded. Full structured extraction and execution cannot be scored for missing-context rows. Dates record the original intake date, not an independently known generation timestamp; this annotation pass is dated separately.

Catalogue requirements are requested properties, not verified facts. Inherited requirements cite the current source turn as the record anchor; their supporting earlier IDs are listed in semantic notes. A scalar colour cannot represent alternatives, and the numeric price field cannot encode strictness or approximate budgets: semantic notes retain OR alternatives and strict/approximate boundaries. These rows must not be judged by field equality alone. Unknown sizing systems, brands, delivery, fit and material facts remain unknown. Size 11 is not converted to 44. The two-T-shirt pack is not assumed to mean two packs.

Compound rows keep one provisional top-level intent because v2 allows only one; notes retain all clauses and primary comparison excludes them. In particular 11/61 require both remove and add; 36/86 request unsupported size changes; 21/43/68/93 include COD; 25 includes WhatsApp; 50 asks a confirmation message; 75 adds a coupon; 98 asks cancellation and cart restoration. 100 is checkout navigation, not submission. Bound Confirmation, off-origin blocking, Sensitive Field exclusion and no uncertain-action replay remain unchanged.

## Cross-source grouping

[The group map](../eval/datasets/capstone-v1/development/combined-group-map.json) covers all 140 records exactly once in 15 connected components. Explicit relation records explain conversation, within-source paraphrase and cross-source semantic links. Component sizes: 104, 12, 5, 3, 3, 2, 2, 2, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1.

Whole conversations and prior paraphrase families are preserved transitively. The large component is a real consequence of related multilingual templates spanning search, cart and checkout; it is not safe to split individual turns to obtain an attractive ratio. This map supersedes the independence assumption behind the earlier 40-record tentative split proposal; it makes no replacement assignment. Historical legacy/prompt overlap still needs a wider audit. Similar intent alone is not independent evidence; conservative merges are labelled as such.
