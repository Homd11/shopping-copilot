# 16 — Validate with people and close the MVP

**What to build:** Run the uncoached five-person study, address the most important evidence-backed usability failures, and publish the reproducible local-MVP results and next bottleneck.

**Blocked by:** 15 — Pass the complete local evaluation gate.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Five participants attempt the filter, cart/checkout guidance, and newest-order tasks without coaching.
- [ ] At least two participants have limited digital confidence.
- [ ] Screen and audio are recorded only with explicit participant consent.
- [ ] Completion, hesitation, trust concerns, misunderstandings, and “what is it doing?” moments are captured consistently.
- [ ] At least ten concrete UX findings are prioritized, and the highest-impact in-scope issues are addressed.
- [ ] Every behaviour change gains regression coverage and the applicable full evaluation gate is rerun.
- [ ] At least three participants report that the Copilot helped them.
- [ ] The final results record includes all three evaluation runs, safety outcomes, steps, latency, cost, interaction traps, study findings, limitations, and the evidence-based next bottleneck.
- [ ] The local MVP is tagged only after the final evidence and regression checks pass.
